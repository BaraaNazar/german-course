📚 Learning Resources

Curated external tools to use alongside this course

Primary Course

🎓

DW · Nicos Weg (A1–B1) Free

Structured video course with interactive exercises. Use as your parallel track: after each lesson here, do the matching Nicos Weg topic. Covers A1, A2, and B1.

📖

Goethe-Institut · Practice Materials Exam prep

Official model tests for all CEFR levels (A1–C2). Use only in the final weeks before each level exam in this course.

Vocabulary

🃏

Anki All levels

The gold standard for spaced repetition. Use the built-in flashcards in this course for daily practice, or export to Anki for mobile review.

🔊

Forvo All levels

Hear how native speakers pronounce any German word. Use this whenever the built-in TTS sounds unclear or when you want to hear regional variations.

📕

Duden B1+

The authoritative German dictionary. Use to check article gender, plural forms, and correct spelling.

Grammar Reference

📐

Lingolia Deutsch A1–B2

Clear grammar explanations with interactive drills. Use when you're stuck on a grammar concept and need extra practice.

🔧

YourDailyGerman B1+

Deep, honest articles on messy German topics (prefixes, adjective endings, modal particles). Great for when the basics click and you want nuance.

Listening

🎧

Easy German A2+

Street interviews with German and English subtitles. Start with "Super Easy German" (A1/A2), progress to regular episodes (B1+). Weekly listening assignments are built into this course.

📰

DW · Langsam gesprochene Nachrichten B1+

Daily news in slow, clear German with transcript. Your bridge from learner German to Tagesschau.

📺

Tagesschau in 100 Sekunden B2+

Native-speed headline news in 100 seconds. From B1-M3 onward, this is your daily listening habit.

Speaking

💬

r/German All levels

Large, well-moderated community. Ask questions, share progress, find study partners.

🗣️

italki / Tandem All levels

Find a tutor or language exchange partner. Weekly conversation practice from A1-M1 onward is recommended.

💡 How to use these resources: Don't use everything at once. Pick one primary course (Nicos Weg), one vocab tool (built-in flashcards), and one listening source (Easy German). Add Goethe practice tests only near exam time. Less is more.
