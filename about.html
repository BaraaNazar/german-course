Our Mission

The goal is genuine real-world communication — understanding and being understood in a German-speaking environment. Passing exams is a checkpoint, never the point.

  • Follow natural-speed conversations, podcasts, films
  • Speak spontaneously in daily life and professional settings
  • Write clear, correct German (messages → emails → essays)
  • Read news, literature, and specialist texts
  • Function independently in a German-speaking country
  • Reach demonstrated C2 competence

How It Works

🧠

Learn by retrieving, not rereading

Every lesson follows: understand → notice → retrieve → produce → feedback → review. Flashcards and quizzes force active recall — the evidence-backed way to build long-term memory.

📊

Spaced repetition built in

Words reappear at precisely timed intervals: today → +1 day → +3 → +7 → +14 → +30 → +60. Struggling words return more often. No manual scheduling needed.

🎯

Small steps, clear wins

Maximum 5-10 new words per lesson. One grammar concept at a time. Each lesson takes 15-30 minutes and gives you one clear, tangible win.

🔊

Audio from day one

Every word has audio. Dialogues are listen-first (translations hidden until you've tried). Pronunciation is precise IPA, never approximated.

📈

7 milestones, one journey

50 words (🌱 First Words) → 100 → 250 → 500 (📙 Conversational) → 1,000 → 2,000 (🎓 Advanced) → 5,000 (👑 Master). Progress is visible and motivating.
