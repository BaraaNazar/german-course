  4. Was bedeutet „das Wörterbuch“?a) son   b) dictionary   c) brother   d) sister

  4. b) dictionary — das Wörterbuch = dictionary.
  5. a) dictionary of national variants — das Variantenwörterbuch = dictionary of national variants.
