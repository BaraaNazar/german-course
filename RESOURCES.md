# German Learning Resources

Knowledge for lessons is drawn from these sources, not from model memory. Every lesson cites what it used. Wisdom = communities where German gets tested in the real world.

## Knowledge

- [CEFR Companion Volume (Council of Europe)](https://www.coe.int/en/web/common-european-framework-reference-languages)
  Canonical descriptors for A1–C2 ("can-do" statements). Use for: level goals, advancement criteria, what each level must demonstrate.
- [Goethe-Institut · Practice materials & exam formats](https://www.goethe.de/en/spr/kup/prf.html)
  Official model tests for A1–C2 (Hören, Lesen, Schreiben, Sprechen). Use for: end-of-level assessments and exam-strategy units only.
- [DW · Nicos Weg (A1, A2, B1)](https://learngerman.dw.com/en/)
  Free structured video course with interactive exercises — the primary companion course for A1–B1. Use for: the assigned 10–15 min parallel practice named in each lesson.
- [DW · Langsam gesprochene Nachrichten](https://www.dw.com/de/deutsch-lernen/nachrichten/s-8030)
  Daily news in slow, clear German with transcript. Use for: B1+ listening bridge to Tagesschau.
- [Tagesschau · In 100 Sekunden](https://www.tagesschau.de/100sekunden)
  Native-speed headline news, ~100 seconds. Use for: B1→B2 daily listening once B1-M3 is reached.
- [Easy German (YouTube / podcast)](https://www.youtube.com/@EasyGerman)
  Street interviews with dual subtitles; "Super Easy German" for beginners. Use for: authentic listening from A2 onward (assigned per lesson with tasks).
- [Duden (duden.de)](https://www.duden.de/)
  Authoritative German dictionary & grammar. Use for: spelling, plural forms, gender, register labels.
- [DWDS (dwds.de)](https://www.dwds.de/)
  Corpus-based dictionary with real usage examples. Use for: collocations and natural contexts at B2+.
- [Lingolia Deutsch](https://deutsch.lingolia.com/en/)
  Clear graded grammar explanations + drills. Use for: targeted extra drills when the error log shows a grammar weakness (A1–B2).
- [YourDailyGerman](https://yourdailygerman.com/)
  Deep, honest articles on messy German (prefixes, adjective endings, particles). Use for: B1+ deep-dives when records show systematic trouble.
- [Wiktionary (de.wiktionary.org)](https://de.wiktionary.org/)
  IPA transcriptions, plurals, audio samples. Use for: precise pronunciation guidance (vowel length, stress) before it enters a lesson.
- [Forvo (forvo.com)](https://forvo.com/languages/de/)
  Native-speaker pronunciation recordings. Use for: hearing a real voice for tricky words when TTS is ambiguous.
