🎧 Pimsleur-Style German

150 lessons · 30 min/day · audio-first · max 5 new words per lesson
How it works
  • Listen to a short conversation (Phase 1)
  • Respond aloud to "How do you say…?" prompts (Phase 2 — the core)
  • Self-check after each response: ✓ correct / ✗ not yet
  • One lesson per day — no skipping, no doubling
  • 5 new words max — everything else is review
